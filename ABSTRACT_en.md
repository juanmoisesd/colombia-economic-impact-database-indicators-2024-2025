# Dataset Abstract

This database compiles key economic indicators and the main influencing factors in Colombia's economy during 2024-2025. The data is based on information obtained from Perplexity.ai and its cited sources, including BBVA Research, Trading Economics, CEIC Data, Deloitte, Itau, Rystad Energy, QCIntel, The Global Economy, Medellin Advisors, and ExchangeRates.

The dataset includes variables such as GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, oil production, exchange rate, poverty, reserves, public debt, trade balance, remittances, foreign direct investment, and fiscal deficit. Additionally, it analyzes factors such as the Petro government's policies, armed conflict, the drug economy, and trade relations with the U.S.
