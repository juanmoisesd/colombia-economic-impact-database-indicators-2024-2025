# Codebook

This codebook describes the variables in the Colombia Economic Impact Database.

| Variable Name | Description | Units | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| GDP Growth | Annual GDP growth rate | % | BBVA Research |
| Inflation | Consumer Price Index change | % | DANE / Trading Economics |
| Unemployment | National unemployment rate | % | DANE |
| Oil Production | Barrels per day | bpd | Rystad Energy |
| Exchange Rate | Official COP/USD rate | COP/USD | ExchangeRates |
| Poverty | Household poverty rate | % | DANE |
| Public Debt | Gross public debt | % of GDP | MinHacienda |
