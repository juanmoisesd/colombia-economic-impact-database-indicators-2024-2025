# Bias and Limitations

- **Source Bias**: The dataset depends on the reporting accuracy of external organizations.
- **AI Extraction**: Use of AI for extraction might introduce minor interpretation errors if not manually verified.
- **Forecasting**: Data for late 2025 may include estimates or forecasts from certain sources.
- **Security Context**: Some economic indicators may be affected by the fluctuating security situation in Colombia, which is difficult to quantify precisely.
