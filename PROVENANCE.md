# Provenance

The data is sourced from multiple public economic reports and compiled using AI-assisted research (Perplexity.ai).

### Data Collection
1. **Source Identification**: Identification of key economic indicators for Colombia 2024-2025.
2. **Data Extraction**: Manual and AI-assisted extraction from official and institutional reports.
3. **Harmonization**: Standardizing units and naming conventions.
4. **Validation**: Comparison between multiple sources to ensure consistency.
