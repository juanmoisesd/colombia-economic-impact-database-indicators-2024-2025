# Colombia Economic Impact Database 2024-2025: Key Indicators and Influencing Factors

Part of the Open Research Collection by Juan Moisés de la Serna Tuya — ORCID: 0000-0002-8401-8018

## Description

This dataset includes: GDP growth rate, inflation rate, unemployment rate, oil production (bpd), official exchange rate (COP/USD), household poverty rate, foreign exchange reserves, public debt (% of GDP), trade balance, remittances, foreign direct investment (FDI), and fiscal deficit.
